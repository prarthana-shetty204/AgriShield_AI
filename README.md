# 🌱 AgriShield AI

### AI-Powered Crop Health Detection & Explainable Diagnosis

AgriShield AI is an intelligent crop health monitoring platform designed to analyze crop images, identify potential health issues, estimate risk and severity, and provide understandable AI-driven recommendations.

The platform focuses on making AI-based crop diagnosis more accessible by combining visual analysis, statistical insights, explainable AI, and decision support in a single interface.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🏠 1. Interactive Home Page

- Clean and modern agricultural AI interface.
- Introduces the AgriShield AI platform.
- Highlights intelligent crop health monitoring.
- Provides a direct **Start AI Diagnosis** action.
- Designed as a single-page application with no login requirement.

---

### 📷 2. AI Crop Diagnosis

Users can upload a crop image for analysis.

The diagnosis workflow is designed to provide:

- Crop health assessment
- Risk percentage
- Severity percentage
- Overall crop health
- AI confidence score
- AI-generated diagnosis

The uploaded image acts as the primary input for the crop health assessment.

---

### 📊 3. Statistical Analysis

AgriShield presents the diagnosis using visual statistical indicators.

The dashboard includes:

- Risk Level (%)
- Severity (%)
- Crop Health (%)
- AI Confidence (%)

A combination chart is used to visualize the relationship between risk, severity, crop health, and confidence.

---

### 🧠 4. Explainable AI

AgriShield is designed to go beyond simply providing a prediction.

The Explainable AI section communicates:

- What the AI detected
- Visual factors contributing to the prediction
- Estimated impact of detected factors
- AI confidence
- Recommended actions

This helps users understand **why** a particular diagnosis was generated.

---

### 🌾 5. Decision Support

Based on the diagnosis, the platform provides actionable guidance such as:

- Monitoring affected crop areas
- Inspecting nearby leaves
- Observing symptom progression
- Performing another scan when required

The objective is to help users make informed decisions rather than relying only on an AI prediction.

---

### 📄 6. Diagnostic Report

The platform is designed to generate a structured diagnostic report containing:

- Final AI diagnosis
- Risk score
- Severity score
- Crop health score
- AI confidence
- Statistical information
- Diagnosis summary
- Recommended action

The report can be exported as a PDF for future reference.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

### Frontend

- React.js
- Vite
- JavaScript
- HTML5
- CSS3
- SVG-based data visualization

### PDF Generation

- jsPDF

### Backend

The frontend is designed to consume the crop diagnosis results generated by the project's backend AI detection system.

Backend-generated results can be mapped into the frontend diagnosis object and displayed across the dashboard, statistics, explainability section, and diagnostic report.
